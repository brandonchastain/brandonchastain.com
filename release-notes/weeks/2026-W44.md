## rss-reader

**New features**
- Clicking a headline opens the post in the reader in place, and the original is one click away at ↗.
- Post lists take vim keys: j and k move, l or Enter opens, m marks read, and ? shows the full list.

**Improvements**
- Open tabs show a Reload banner when a newer build deploys, instead of running the old version until a hard refresh.

**Reliability**
- A stalled feed no longer goes unnoticed: an anonymous health check reports how old the newest stored post is and how many feeds are failing.
- The replica keeps three hours of history instead of 24, so it should shrink from 8.7 GB to roughly 0.9 to 1.4 GB, at the cost of a point-in-time restore window of three to four hours instead of a day.

**Fixes**
- Collapsing an open post no longer marks it read, so a mark-unread made while it was open sticks.

**Infrastructure**
- Sign-in cookie keys now live in blob storage, and the first boot copied them over, so nobody was signed out.
- Self-hosters must now put the database on a mounted volume, since the whole-file backup is gone.
