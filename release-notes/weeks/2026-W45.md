## rss-reader

**Fixes**
- The first fetch on a new account waits for registration and retries, so the first visit no longer comes up empty.
- Deleting your account signs you out. Before, the page quietly created a fresh account with starter feeds.
- Cancelling GitHub sign-in returns you to the app instead of an error page.

**Improvements**
- Post rows are tighter and even, each 81 px tall at desktop width, down from about 104 px.
- Reader actions sit on one line under the body, and row controls are icon buttons that fill green when on.
- The privacy page names Google, Microsoft and GitHub as sign-in providers and links their privacy policies.

**Reliability**
- Blob replication runs every 30 minutes instead of every 30 seconds, so a hard crash can lose up to 30 minutes of writes. Planned deploys still lose none.
- A timeline that fails to load shows "Couldn't load posts" with a Retry button, after retrying transient errors.
- Two requests creating the same new account at once no longer fail with a server error.

**Infrastructure**
- Background storage work runs less often, with replica checks every 5 minutes and handover polls every 10 seconds, cutting blob-storage calls and letting more seconds bill as idle. A deploy can now wait up to 10 seconds before handing over.
